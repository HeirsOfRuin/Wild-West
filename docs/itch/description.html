Dead or Alive

An outlaw gang in Missouri, 1866-1882.

The war is over and you are not finished with it. Make your outlaw, ride out with two or three of your own people, and rob the banks, stagecoaches and trains of western Missouri while the counties still take your side. Every season asks the same question: one more job, or is it time to quit?

The game

  • One season a turn. Scout a target, give each man a role (inside, street, horses), ride, and get home before the posse does. The odds you see are the odds the game rolls.
  • 6 counties, each with its own sympathy, sheriff and heat. Rob where you are loved and hide where you are owed; spend on horses, guns and safe houses; bribe a sheriff or back his rival at the election.
  • Your men have their own loyalty, moods and stories, and you cannot see what they think. The reward on your head grows, the Pinkertons come after 1874, and any of your friends might be the one who sells you.
  • Killed, captured or betrayed ends it. To win, marry, put money by, let the gang go, and live quietly as Mr. Howard until the world forgets you. A careful player gets out about one game in five.

About it

  • Plays in the browser, on a phone or a computer. Also a download: one file that opens in any browser and plays offline (not Safari: it will not keep saves from a file), and an Android app.
  • Saves in your browser. The save code under News moves a game between devices.
  • A family code: an outlaw who retires leaves one, for a sequel set in 1933 that is in development.
  • Free. No account, no ads, no analytics. The page asks Google Fonts for its typefaces; without them it plays the same in your system's fonts.

Made with AI assistance. I designed the game and its systems and directed, edited and play-tested it; the code, the text and the art were generated with an AI model to that design.
